Runny Hound

Go, Greyhounds! Leap through Bethlehem, the Christmas City.

How to play

  • Space / ↑ / tap: jump. Hold for a higher leap.
  • ↓: duck under flying stars, or drop faster mid-air.
  • M mute all sound · N toggle music · P pause

Meet the obstacles

All graphics and sounds are generated in code. No image or audio files are used.
